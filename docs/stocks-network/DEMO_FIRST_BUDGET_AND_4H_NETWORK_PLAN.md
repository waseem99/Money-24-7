**Network SECOND:** a 3–4-hour scheduled US-financial television block, with up to four stable fictional AI avatars in multiple programme formats; live and pre-recorded segments, grounded data retrieval, human editorial authorisation behind the scenes, recorded interviews/interactions, a shared playout feed, fallback/slates, private archive, monetisation/rights and Tamasha ingest. Do not promise 3–4 hours/day of completely NEW humanlike real-time avatar faces until vendor soak/limits and economics are proven.

### Demo gates
D0: design still and separated independent source portraits locked, single fictional person per 1920×1080 neutral mouth portrait; source/likeness rights.
D1: provider account tier/fictional image-to-avatar eligibility, **two** distinct custom 1080p assets, streaming LITE, commercial TV recording/reuse and exact price in writing.
D2: 10–15s single-host motion proof from a **real** streaming avatar, not photo slides; inspect cheeks, eyelashes, glasses, teeth, mouth/head alignment, perspective and shadows.
D3: second speaker source + 30–45s natural camera handoff. If dual simultaneous shared-desk doesn't pass, use close camera cuts and chart-driven edit, not counterfeit two-person gesturing.
D4: approved US-market script+TTS/voice references with checks; source-driven overlays.
D5: actual 165s client demo MP4 + thumbnail/contact QA; complete H.264/AAC decoder playback and full human-motion shot/timecode review, zero P0/P1 open defects, editorial rights and signed stakeholder review hash.
D6: upload a private review link and provide high-quality client handoff with demo disclaimers, source note and platform roadmap. Only after D5+creative approval should any large-scale digital-avatar generation/streaming begin.

**Creative look standards:** 1080p at 30p unless ingest spec says otherwise; closeups not over-zoomed, premium blue single newsroom, thin lower thirds, 1 small corner brand, charts sourced, one moving ticker, synced AI mouth speech within 100ms *where measurable*, clean hand gesture/desk shot (no frozen fake listening), 48kHz AAC audio and professional loudness; system is not released if lips/background betray a fake source.

## AUTOMATION VS HUMAN QA
AI agents can continuously gather US market calendars, filings, prices (under permitted feed), cluster stories, propose scripts, generate synthetic speech/audio, schedule and edit deterministic chart segments, identify broken lip sync, verify timestamps, assemble and caption clip packages, and run non-billable checks. **Human editors/technical directors remain off-screen** to sign factual/rights/creative reviews and supervise live interactive prompts. Never give a presenter model discretionary control to invent/share financial claims live without retrieving approved facts.
