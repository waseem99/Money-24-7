# Stocks editorial graphics benchmark

26-second motion study and three 1920x1080 design frames. This is a new design proposal, not an approved channel identity or a claim of parity with any network. It retains the approved studio footage and introduces an off-white/ink/teal/lime palette, condensed headline typography, reserved presenter area, separate data rail, continuous illustrative crawl, chart units and editorial takeaway.

Sequence: 0–4 seconds program identity; 4–12 studio and data rail; 12–22 animated earnings comparison; 22–26 next-program identity. Only the studio interval carries existing presenter dialogue; original synthesized stings accompany the identities. Chart interval is a graphics demonstration without new narration. No paid avatar or video generation.

Illustrative dataset: period 1 revenue $100m, operating profit $12m, margin 12%; period 2 revenue $120m, operating profit $9.6m, margin 8%. Revenue growth +20%, operating-profit change -20%, margin change -4 percentage points. These are hypothetical figures, labeled on screen. Revenue bars use a zero baseline and linear common scale. Margin panel is a labeled two-point comparison, not a market time series.

Code renderer: production/broadcast-sample/graphics-benchmark.py. Current implementation is 2D motion and typography; dimensional Blender title production remains a later refinement rather than a claimed completed feature.

Review criteria: headline and ticker legibility, presenter face clearance, correct scales/units, consistent hierarchy, restrained animation and clear attribution. Studio monitor replacement and live market feeds are not implemented by this benchmark. After direction approval, externalize all story content into a validated data contract and extend the approved templates to other shows.
